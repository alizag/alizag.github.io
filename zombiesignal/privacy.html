Zombie Signal: Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

Zombie Signal is a game made by one person. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts of its own, and it doesn't sell or share your data. The only things that leave your device are what online co-op needs to work, described below.

What stays on your device

Your progress, records and settings (including your touch layout and the name you pick for co-op) are saved on your device only. Deleting the game deletes them.

Co-op on iPhone, iPad and Android: Epic Online Services

On phones and tablets, co-op runs on Epic Online Services (EOS), a service from Epic Games, Inc. You don't need an Epic account and you never sign in to one. Here is what happens:

  • When the game starts, it signs in to EOS with an ID that EOS makes for your device. EOS receives that ID and your device's model name (for example "iPhone 13"). This happens whether or not you play co-op, so that co-op is ready when you open it.
  • When you host or join a squad, EOS's servers keep the lobby and its squad code so your friends can find it, and may relay the game between players when the devices can't connect directly.
  • Your squad sees the name you type in Co-op, and the game sends your moves to them during a run.
  • Your IP address is seen by Epic's servers and, when the devices connect directly, by the other players' devices. Any internet game needs this to connect you.

We use this only to run co-op. We don't see your IP address or any other details, and we don't combine any of it with other data. Epic processes it to provide the service, may keep basic technical data (such as error codes) to keep it running, and may process it on servers in the United States. Epic's privacy policy: legal.epicgames.com/epicgames/privacy-policy.

If you'd like the ID EOS keeps for your device deleted, contact us (below) and we'll ask Epic to delete it.

Co-op on PC and Mac: Steam

On a computer, co-op runs through Steam. The players in your lobby see your Steam name. Valve's privacy policy applies to Steam: store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement.

Purchases

The unlock on phones is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment; the game only learns whether the unlock is owned, never your payment details. Their privacy policies apply to purchases.

Beta testing

If you test the game through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports, basic usage information and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send with us, under Apple's TestFlight terms. We use them only to fix and improve the game, and we don't share them with anyone.

Children

Zombie Signal isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them. The game asks no one for their age, name or contact details; the device ID above is used only to run co-op.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
