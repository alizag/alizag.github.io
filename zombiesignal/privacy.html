Zombie Signal: Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

Zombie Signal is a game. It does not collect, sell or share your personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts of its own, and it sends nothing to us.

What stays on your device

Your progress, records and settings (including your touch layout) are saved on your device only. Deleting the game deletes them.

Game Center (iPhone and iPad)

Signing in, friend invites and co-op matches are run by Apple's Game Center. The players in your squad see your Game Center nickname, and the game sends your moves to them during a match. We do not receive your Apple ID, your contacts or your friends list. Apple's privacy policy applies to Game Center: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Steam (PC and Mac)

On a computer, co-op runs through Steam. The players in your lobby see your Steam name. Valve's privacy policy applies to Steam.

Beta testing

If you test the game through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports, basic usage information and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send with us, under Apple's TestFlight terms. We use them only to fix and improve the game, and we don't share them with anyone.

Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
